# Business Requirements Document

## Executive Summary
Purpose of the initiative and the outcome sought.

## Business Objectives
Numbered objectives, each with a measurable target if the sources provide one.

## Scope
What is in scope and what is explicitly out of scope.

## Stakeholders
Roles and what each needs from this initiative.

## Functional Requirements
Numbered FR-001, FR-002. Each is atomic and testable, with its priority.

## Non-Functional Requirements
Security, performance, availability, compliance, each with a checkable criterion.

## Business Rules
Rules the solution must enforce.

## Assumptions
Assumptions made because the sources were silent.

## Risks and Mitigations
Risk, impact, and mitigation.

## Open Questions
Items that need a decision from the business.
